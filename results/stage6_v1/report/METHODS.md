# Stage 6: physical and evaluation conventions

This document describes the executed implementation. Completion counts and scientific decisions belong to `REPORT.md` and the evidence ledger, not to this methods description.

## Model, observables and reference scope

The circuit problem is the periodic Pauli Hamiltonian
`H=-sum ZZ(i,i+1)+kappa sum ZZ(i,i+2)-h sum X(i)`, with wire 0 the most significant state-vector bit. The MPS problem is separately open-boundary, with the omitted end couplings explicitly accounted for. A global Hadamard maps the XX/Z convention of Cea et al. to this project's ZZ/X convention. No Computational Track hardware graph is imposed; actual two-qubit gates use direct nearest- or next-nearest-neighbour links.

All distances and all `q=2*pi*k/N` are retained. The structure factor includes diagonal self-correlations and hence its `1/N` background. The ideal four-period antiphase has `m_(pi/2)^2=1/2`. Correlation and structure factor are Fourier-related, not independent evidence. The h=0 classical incoherent mixture remains distinct from positive-field pure states. Missing h=0 fidelity intervals and the h-midpoint axis are not filled or relabelled.

R0 labels are analytic limits or explicitly qualified phase interiors. RN contains finite-size observables, response and fidelity features, higher moments, sector gaps and grid intervals. Rlarge OBC findings remain tied to their actual sizes and sample locations. Reference scope v3 preserves original labels but separates multi-size pattern proxies from physically supported interiors; its low-field extension requires both the enumerated classical defect energy and strong N12/16 order/Binder trends. This is not a rigorous perturbation bound. Neither detector outputs on ED nor literature curves supply independent full-plane ground-truth labels.

The reference eigensolver uses the appropriate even-spin-flip, translation-invariant sector and records residuals and within-sector gaps. For finite N and h>0, the computational-basis off-diagonal entries are nonpositive and connect the full hypercube. The Perron-Frobenius argument therefore gives a unique positive ground vector, invariant under the spin-flip and translation permutations; the chosen sector is justified for this model rather than imposed on the variational output. This prevents arbitrary vectors in a nearly degenerate full-space low-energy group from generating spurious fidelity peaks. It does not change the physical Hamiltonian or authorize enlarging the VQE target subspace. Same-size ED is used for circuit verification; large-size results are not transferred as small-size labels. The archived reused-vector residual was independently recomputed for261 N8 reference archives: maximum2.15e-14, with no numerical-ambiguity change; the original record residual refers to the preceding new-sector solution. See reference_reuse_residual_audit.json.

## Domain-wall construction and charged gate implementation

For `d_i=(1-Z_i Z_(i+1))/2`, substitution gives
`H_Z=N(kappa-1)+(2-4*kappa)sum d_i+4*kappa sum d_i d_(i+1)`.
At kappa=1/2 this reduces to `-N/2+2 sum d_i d_(i+1)`. Periodic spin strings have an even total wall count. The N physical degrees of freedom are encoded as `y_0=b_0`, `y_j=b_(j-1) XOR b_j`; the closing wall is the parity of y_1 through y_(N-1). Sequential CNOT(0,1), CNOT(1,2), ..., CNOT(N-2,N-1) decodes to physical spins. These N-1 CNOTs are charged and noisy.

The wall references are uniform, empty and alternating wall strings, with y_0 in |+>. The pool contains Y on each nonzero wall wire and YZ, ZY, YX, XY on separation-one/two pairs of those wires. Rotations are `exp(-i theta P)` and use a Pauli-basis change, CNOT parity computation, RZ(2 theta), uncomputation and inverse basis changes. They can change wall number, unlike a constrained wall-hopping mixer. The final energy always uses the full physical H, with no projection to the no-adjacent-wall subspace. The construction is spin-flip even but is not assumed translation invariant. No StatePrep or hidden symmetry projection is used.

C0 uses the physical reference/pool with greedy gradient-per-CNOT growth. C1 probes the top four affordable candidates, optimizes each for at most 35 iterations, then fully optimizes the best proposal. C2 and C3 use the wall basis with the corresponding greedy and top-four strategies. Every full optimization uses analytic state-vector adjoint gradients, L-BFGS-B, at most 400 iterations, ftol=1e-12 and gtol=1e-7. The 160-step parameter cap is explicit; zero-CNOT one-qubit rotations are not unlimited. Intermediate 64/96/128-CNOT checkpoints and every probe are retained.

The frozen H6 rule uses the union of three C0 and three C3 references in kappa in [.4,.6], h<=.35, and C0 elsewhere. It selects one literal circuit within 1e-6 energy per site of the lowest energy, then minimizes CNOT count, then energy. It does not average states, project them or select using ED error. Shared C0 computations are counted once in actual execution cost and separately in standalone search comparisons. Historical B3 retains its original stopping and energy tie rules; it is not silently renamed C0.

Fixed-structure energy restarts use three new seeds and at most 2000 iterations. Development-only fidelity fits are oracle diagnostics and cannot supply confirmation circuits. Successful oracle fitting supports an optimization explanation; unsuccessful fitting does not prove mathematical inexpressibility. Lie-algebra connectivity at unrestricted depth is likewise not a finite-budget guarantee.

## Isolation and evaluation denominators

Development, validation and confirmation coordinate hashes are frozen separately. The 96-point confirmation set has 48 low-field points, 24 antiphase-band points and 24 interior controls. These are new-coordinate, spatial-block interpolation tests, not an unseen-phase generalization claim: the historical public grid already spans the domain. Candidate generation and selection do not load confirmation ED states or labels. Confirmation scoring is unsealed only after generation completes. Preparation methods and detector settings remain frozen thereafter. Mitigation has a separate registered freeze: after the p0 confirmation assessment, the estimator is selected using validation-noise data only, before any noisy confirmation evaluation. This staged procedure is not described as a single simultaneous blind unsealing of every analysis.

State quality, observable quality and phase-task performance are separate. Original thresholds are unchanged: energy error <=1e-3/site, maximum C/SF and Mx errors <=.02, fidelity >=.99. Optimizer termination is a different field. All failures and ambiguous references remain in denominators. RN feature matching uses the same named feature and fixed window; arbitrary nearest-peak matching is forbidden. Requested, reference-resolvable and algorithm-estimable coverage are reported separately. Grid intervals are not statistical confidence intervals. Maps use cell-intersection area, not refined point counts; missing coordinates retain their requested area.

## Noise, information and mitigation

Each literal CNOT, including reference preparation, wall decoding and 1/3/5 folding, is followed on its target by `(1-p)rho+p/3*(XrhoX+YrhoY+ZrhoZ)`, for p=0,.01,.05. No terminal replacement channel is used. Exact density simulation is checked against the pure circuit at p=0. N8 spectra are checked for positivity; N12 relies on the verified CPTP construction plus trace/Hermiticity checks, not a falsely claimed full diagonalization.

The six-level comparison separates ideal ED information, ED finite measurements, circuit p0, noisy exact expectations, noisy finite measurements and mitigation. The ED layers are classical ideal-information references, not free quantum state preparation. Quantum trace distance and classical X/Z-setting-plus-bitstring total variation are compared on fixed pairs, with TV<=trace distance checked. Known-template likelihood discrimination is an offline binary oracle diagnostic, not a trained phase classifier or a phase-wide accuracy bound.

D4 uses conjugate-folded structure factors with multiplicity weighting, removal of the self-correlation constant, Mx and a regularized covariance metric. It does not duplicate C and SF. D5 uses the even-wall-count histogram, no-adjacent-wall probability and Mx from the same X/Z counts. Both are trained only on declared development references; p, coordinates and circuit resources are not detector inputs. The frozen training set includes v1 multi-size pattern labels subsequently downgraded to RN proxies in reporting scope v3. The original training metadata wording "independent-labelled" is too broad; `detector_training_scope_audit.json` gives the exact physical/proxy counts. No retraining follows this audit, and proxy agreement is never reported as independent thermodynamic accuracy. Full-state D2 access is reported separately from local-measurement budgets.

D4/D5 have separately calibrated N8 and N12 feature prototypes and distance/margin settings, both frozen at the original method-freeze time using the same declared development/validation coordinates and the corresponding size's clean ED features. N12 detector evaluation is therefore not zero-shot transfer of an N8 detector. The multi-size reference records explicitly list N8/N12/N16; their physical-interior/proxy scope is preserved. In contrast, N12 candidate pools, search and energy/resource selection follow the frozen scaling rule without N12 test-error tuning.

Raw, linear ZNE, quadratic Richardson ZNE and signed symmetry verification are compared on common coordinates. The reported primary MSE is the fixed mean squared error over the full vector [all C, all SF, Mx]; C/SF are Fourier-related, so this error norm is not a count of independent physical evidence. D4 uses a separate nonredundant feature distance. SV uses `(O+OP)/(1+P)`; it does not repair translation momentum. The two ZNE fits use identical saved 1/3/5 counts, and no p0 or ED value enters extrapolation. A total of 10k or 100k shots per coordinate/p/method is split among all settings/scales, with 32 repetitions. The 32-repetition mean costs 3.2 million shots at the 100k setting and is not a 100k image. Quadratic ZNE adds no samples to linear ZNE. No unstable or nonphysical extrapolate is clipped.

Seed families are shared by exact coordinate, N, total budget, repetition, fold and measurement basis, omitting arm and p. NumPy multinomial algorithms can consume different random draws at different probabilities: shared seed families do not mean identical categorical events. All detectors receive the same archived counts. Gate-weighted shot cost and extra measurement-basis rotations are saved.

## Large-size slice and uncertainty

The main MPS slice is kappa=.8, initially h=.1:.05:.8 at N64/chi128, with controlled N96/128, chi128/256 refinements and targeted larger N/chi checks. Actual solver stopping, bond agreement, initial-state agreement and size trends are independent requirements. Unconverged runs remain available. The stopping check is recomputed from raw TeNPy sweep statistics; the historical incorrect relative-energy flag is preserved alongside its correction.

Signed raw and connected correlations use recorded central midpoint pair sets with endpoints away from edges and r<=N/3. Ordered-platform, power-law and exponential oscillatory models use the same data, comparable parameter counts and contiguous-distance held-out blocks. Their fit scores are descriptive predictive checks, not independent-distance p-values. OBC entropy uses a freely fitted c/6 chord formula and a separately reported oscillatory correction.

Friedel K is fitted to the transverse X profile using the primary paper's Eq8 and Appendix B2, not renamed from a ZZ decay exponent. The original seven-point coefficients, two frozen edge windows and alternative raw-background sensitivity are all retained. A small residual, c near1 or an incommensurate peak alone is insufficient. Larger-N contradictions block a supported-floating conclusion. Even chi convergence cannot exclude a gapped state with correlation length much larger than N. Supported discrete samples and brackets must not be promoted to a continuous thermodynamic phase band.

## Reproducibility

All new outputs are under `results/stage6_v1`; historical scientific inputs are protected. Cache keys include exact coordinates and relevant model, gate, parameter, backend and measurement configuration hashes. Parameters and literal gate lists can reconstruct selected states. Execution, historical reuse and unrun implementations remain distinct. The packaging protocol requires a clean-extraction check with an external locked interpreter; the actual outcome is recorded separately in deliverables/STAGE6_CLEAN_EXTRACTION_CHECK.json. Archives exclude virtual environments, credentials and fonts.

## Supplemental response interpretation

The predeclared peak rule selects the largest interior peak and records endpoint maxima separately. The later descriptive endpoint audit does not replace that rule: it reveals when a formally matched interior peak coexists with a stronger endpoint response. Such a match is not promoted to reliable physical-boundary recovery. Four explicitly exploratory same-coordinate growth-checkpoint controls compare actual saved gates and parameters at p=0,.01,.05; they isolate the combined preparation choice from a change in Hamiltonian, not a single-gate causal effect. Neither the controls nor the endpoint audit retrain the detector or change confirmation selections.

## Reporting-only numerical and storage corrections

Map reference comparisons normalize values only within sixteen machine epsilons of existing region endpoints. For example, a generated kappa of 0.30000000000000004 must not silently lose the intended endpoint inclusion at 0.3. The full-precision Hamiltonian coordinates, cache keys, frozen detector outputs and confirmation evaluations are unchanged. The original comparison and corrected map-reference scope are both saved. This is an endpoint arithmetic correction, not a newly tuned physical threshold or detector improvement; see `reference_atlas/map_endpoint_roundoff_protocol.json`.

Compact batch indices link to the complete immutable per-execution metadata with a SHA-256 hash, including full CNOT order and measurement settings. They do not discard the underlying gate or count records. The resource ledger distinguishes complete literal coordinate tuples from binary-pair-only records; rounded coordinate groups are explicitly for reporting and never form cache keys. Stability has twelve physical coordinates and three seeds, hence thirty-six coordinate-seed assessment rows: twenty-four new stability rows plus twelve reused seed631 confirmation rows. The reused rows are not new stability executions.
